# Artwork

`survey-overview.png` and `timeline.webp` are web renderings of the survey authors'
original manuscript figures (`F01_teaser_B_video_bridge_v2.pdf` and
`F02_field_timeline-5.pdf`). They are included with the authors' authorization.
The timeline is lossless and retains the original labels, colors, and layout.

The four symbols in `icons/` match the paper's eye, pencil, exchange, and gamepad
glyphs and ink colors. Their outlines come from Dave Gandy's
[Font Awesome 4.7.0](https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/tree/v4.7.0),
whose font is licensed under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/).
No complete font is distributed here.

The [companion website](https://vid-gen.github.io/video-world-model-survey/)
includes the full visual guide and additional original figures. Third-party
research remains credited through the bibliographic records and source links.
